Neighborhood Health Forecasting with Spatial Deep Learning

An example item showing objective, data, method, and contribution.

Overview

Goal: Build an interpretable forecasting model for short-term community health risk at fine spatial resolution.

Data: Historical health indicators, mobility intensity, land-use context, and local weather signals aggregated to neighborhood grids.

Method: A spatiotemporal deep learning pipeline that combines geographic adjacency, time-series dynamics, and feature attribution to explain key drivers.

Expected Contribution: Earlier risk detection and better targeting of prevention resources for vulnerable areas.
