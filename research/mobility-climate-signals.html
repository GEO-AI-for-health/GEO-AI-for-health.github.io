Integrating Mobility and Climate Signals in Urban Health Analytics

An example item showing motivation, data fusion, and practical value.

Overview

Goal: Quantify how movement patterns and local climate variability jointly influence short-term urban health indicators.

Data: Aggregated mobility traces, weather observations, and neighborhood-level health metrics over matched time windows.

Method: Multi-source feature fusion with temporal modeling and sensitivity analysis to identify dominant drivers across areas.

Expected Contribution: Better early warning insights and stronger evidence for location-specific public health planning.
