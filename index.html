Research agenda

Spatial intelligence for healthier environments

01

Geospatial big data mining

Extracting spatial, temporal, and behavioral knowledge from large-scale mobility, sensing, and urban data.

02

GeoAI and AI4S

Developing explainable spatial AI and scientific discovery methods for complex environment-health systems.

03

Multimodal geospatial learning

Fusing audio, imagery, sensor observations, mobility traces, and contextual geographic information.

04

Urban world models

Learning generative representations of urban environments, including cross-view generation and spatial reasoning.

05

Dynamic exposure and health

Connecting daily mobility and environmental exposure with momentary stress, behavior, and mental well-being.
