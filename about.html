Researching people, place, and health through GeoAI.

Profile

Linsen Wang (王林森)

I am a PhD candidate at the Institute of Space and Earth Information Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Professor Mei-Po Kwan.

My doctoral research is titled Decoding Dynamic Urban Audio-Visual Environments: A Geo-AI and Mobile Sensing Framework for Uncovering Noise-Greenery Interactions and Compound Effects on Mental Well-Being. I am particularly interested in moving beyond static residential measures toward models that reflect people's daily mobility, multimodal environmental experience, and momentary responses.

My research has been published in leading journals including Landscape and Urban Planning, Annals of the American Association of Geographers, Cities, and Social Science & Medicine, among others.
